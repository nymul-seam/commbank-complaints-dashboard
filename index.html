      About this dashboard: KPI definitions and assumptions

      KPIs

      • Total complaints: count of complaint records matching all active filters.
      • Timely response rate: share of complaints where Timely response = Yes.
      • Disputed rate: share of complaints where Consumer disputed = Yes.
      • Resolved with relief: complaints closed with monetary or non-monetary relief, as a share of closed complaints (in-progress cases excluded from the denominator).
      • Open cases: complaints with Status = In progress.
      • Top issue: the most frequent Issue in the current selection.
      • When filters are active, each KPI also shows the all-data figure for comparison.

      Assumptions

      • Time is based on Date received. Quarters are calendar quarters. 2011 contains only two records (data starts Aug 2011), so its low count reflects coverage, not performance.
      • Status and Company response match one-to-one in this data (every "In progress" response has Status "In progress"), so open cases use Status.
      • Panels cross-filter: each chart applies every filter except its own dimension, so you can still see and switch between alternatives. The trend and annual charts both ignore the time filters and instead highlight the selected period. Picking a year clears a picked quarter and vice-versa.
      • The map uses simplified state outlines drawn for this dashboard (no external map data). ACT is shown as a marker because it is too small to click at this scale.
      • Postcode, Sub-product and Complaint ID are not visualised. All figures come straight from the 467 rows in the source sheet; nothing is estimated.
